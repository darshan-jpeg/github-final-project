# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple web-based calculator that calculates simple interest and the total amount based on the principal amount, interest rate, and time period.

## Features

- Calculate Simple Interest
- Calculate Total Amount
- Simple and user-friendly interface
- Instant calculation

## Formula

Simple Interest:

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Total Amount:

A = P + SI

Where:

- P = Principal Amount
- R = Rate of Interest (%)
- T = Time Period (Years)
- SI = Simple Interest
- A = Total Amount

## Example

For a principal amount of ₹10,000, an interest rate of 5%, and a time period of 2 years:

SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

SI = ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹10,000 + ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹11,000
